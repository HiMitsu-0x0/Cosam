COSAM

A C++ remake inspired by Terry A. Davis and TempleOS.

Inspired by the song "COSAM", its place in TempleOS, and Terry Davis's unique way of expressing his ideas through music and code.

Built with C++ and SDL2.

Build
g++ -std=c++17 main.cpp -o cosam -lSDL2

An independent community project, not affiliated with Terry A. Davis or TempleOS.
